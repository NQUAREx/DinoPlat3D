### Surface footsteps (request5)

- Official pack downloaded and its enclosed CC0 notice checked on2026-10-21: [Kenney Impact Sounds ZIP](https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/impact-sounds/87b4ddecda-1677589768/kenney_impact-sounds.zip). Source takes `footstep_snow_000..002.ogg`, `footstep_concrete_000..002.ogg`, `footstep_grass_000..002.ogg`, `footstep_wood_000..002.ogg` live in `art/audio/impact/`; the original pack `License.txt` is retained there. These are actual supplied sound files, not generated footsteps. Snow's granular crunch is adapted as sand; concrete supplies rock/grit. No claim that Kenney recorded these specifically on desert sand or lava.
- Twelve short cues (`sand0..2`, `stone0..2`, `grass0..2`, `wood0..2`) provide three different source takes per texture. Ten profiles use distinct low-pass/rate/gain combinations: loose sand, packed sand, dry grit; porous rock, gravel, dense basalt; grass, damp turf, dry grass; wooden planks. Covers are sound interpretations of the three existing visual cover indices, not new terrain metadata. A separate step sequence avoids pickup-driven repetition; each step has subtle ±2% rate and ±4% gain variation. Eight reusable low-pass nodes are allocated only at unlock; effects reset filtering when stealing a channel. Collision/death still exclusively use channels6/7. The never-played decoder sync marker and one-time offset correction remain.
- Runtime integration uses `new GameSound(events, () => surfaceId)` at each Step. IDs0–8 are biome*3+cover (Desert0/Lava1/Meadow2);9 is a wooden bridge and overrides underlying biome. Invalid IDs fall back to sand. The callback samples terrain at the dinosaur contact position. Step keeps its numeric planted-foot payload. Existing one-argument construction defaults to sand.
- Audio checks: `npx tsx --test tests/sound.test.ts` checks all profiles/takes, payload preservation, eight-source bound, reserved impacts, codec padding adjustment, one decode, mute/suspend/disposal and actual48kHz PCM budget/audibility/clipping. `npx tsx --test --test-name-pattern='audio ships' tests/menu-camera.test.ts` checks the existing shipping contract with22 cues. `PLAYWRIGHT_SKIP_VALIDATE_HOST_REQUIREMENTS=1 node tests/sound-browser.mjs` runs only an isolated audio fixture: Chromium and WebKit passed real48kHz decoding, ten audible/unclipped offline renders, reserved impacts and suspend cleanup. Chromium decoded4,091,984bytes, WebKit4,102,680bytes; respective marker corrections −0.021ms and +50.104ms. It does not launch the game or run route/mobile integration tests.

- Runtime atlas: `src/assets/terrains.webp`, **529 386 bytes**, 10×10 cells of 128×128 (three unused). It decodes once into **97 DataArrayTexture layers**: 64 base variants, one shared stone, 16 entries and 16 rotated exits. Independent mipmaps prevent layer bleeding. - A base tile now covers **2.5×2.5m**; painted strips are 1.25m in / 2.5m stone / 1.25m out. Nearest magnification, LINEAR_MIPMAP_LINEAR minification to reduce subpixel shimmer. Unchanged authored art and one opaque albedo sample, no source repacking, biome overlays or N² connectors.
- `TerrainSurface` is project-authored indexed terrain, **224m /28×28 cells /1568 triangles**, flat with height-provider/decoration hooks. World-anchored cells/wrapped shader hashes preserve tile identity; flat rebases never re-upload geometry/normals. `TerrainField` defines reversible seeded 500×500m regions in both axes, with equal neighbour-distinct palettes. Painted connectors also rotate 90° at X boundaries, with shared-stone junctions; the atlas/art is unchanged. Standard Lambert pipeline, no normal/bump/displacement/PBR/postprocessing maps.
- The retired planar voxel-silhouette/stencil shadow path is removed. - No visible moon asset/mesh. One virtual directional-light slot moves continuously through day/night with interpolated colour/intensity, plus a hemisphere ambient. The dinosaur uses a project-authored 16-triangle contact ellipse, animated from its interpolated height/yaw/duck; no texture or additional shadow target. It is an approximate footprint, not an exact silhouette.
